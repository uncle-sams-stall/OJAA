About Uncle Sam's

Uncle Sam’s combines delicious flavor with environmental stewardship. Every dish is crafted from locally sourced produce, zero artificial syrups, and served exclusively in natural banana leaves and paper containers.

100% Sustainable Packaging

We have eliminated single-use plastics from our entire service workflow in favor of fully compostable, earth-friendly materials.

Natural Banana Leaves

Fresh organic banana leaves serve as waterproof, heat-resistant food wraps that naturally preserve humidity and aroma while decomposing 100% naturally within days.

Kraft Paper Boxes

Unbleached, recyclable paper boxes keep meal items warm and sturdy without requiring plastic wax coatings.

Plant-Lined Paper Cups

Our cold beverage cups are made from FSC-certified paper lined with biodegradable plant-starch (PLA) inner walls.

UN SDG Goals We Solve

Uncle Sam's actively contributes to global targets under the United Nations Sustainable Development Framework:

SDG 12: Responsible Consumption & Production

Using banana leaves, compostable paper boxes, and local farm sourcing ensures near-zero plastic and organic landfill waste.

SDG 13: Climate Action

Shortening ingredient supply chains to local suppliers drastically reduces food transport greenhouse gas emissions.
